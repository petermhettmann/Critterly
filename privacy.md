# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** April 24, 2026
**Last updated:** April 24, 2026

Critterly ("we", "us", "the app") is a pet care tracking app published as a personal project. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app collects, how it uses that information, and your rights.

We've tried to write this in plain English. If anything is unclear, email us at **support@critterly.app** and we'll explain.

---

## The short version

- Critterly stores your pet care data **only on your device**. It never leaves your phone.
- We do not have a server, and we do not have your data.
- We do not collect analytics, telemetry, or crash reports.
- We do not show ads (yet — see "Future features" below).
- We do not have user accounts.
- The app requests notification permission so it can remind you about pet care tasks. Notifications are scheduled and delivered locally by your phone's operating system.

---

## What data the app stores

When you use Critterly, the app stores the following information **on your device only**, in a local SQLite database:

- Pet profiles you create: name, species, breed, photo, birthday, sex, microchip ID, vet name and phone, archive status
- Care events you log: type, title, notes, date, time, structured details (e.g. food amount, walk distance, dosage)
- Reminders you set: schedule, frequency, times, end date, active status, notification preferences
- Medications: name, dosage, schedule
- Weight entries: weight, unit, date, notes
- App preferences: theme, default weight unit, haptic feedback, onboarding state

This data is stored using your phone's standard sandboxed app storage. Other apps cannot read it. We cannot read it.

If you delete the app, this data is deleted along with it. You can also clear all data manually via Settings → Reset all data.

---

## What we DO NOT collect

To be unambiguous, Critterly does **not** collect, transmit, or store any of the following:

- Your name, email, phone number, or any identifying personal information
- Your location (the app does not request location permission)
- Your contacts
- Crash logs, error reports, or telemetry
- Analytics on how you use the app
- Advertising identifiers (IDFA, GAID)

Critterly has no backend server. There is nowhere for your data to go even if we wanted it.

---

## Permissions the app requests

**Notifications.** Critterly schedules local reminders for the care tasks you create. The notifications are scheduled and delivered by your phone's operating system. We do not have a notification server and we do not push remote notifications.

**Photos / Camera.** When you add a photo for a pet, the app uses your phone's photo picker or camera. The photo is copied to the app's private storage and stored locally only.

If you decline either permission, the app continues to work — you just won't get reminders or be able to add photos.

---

## Children's privacy

Critterly is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. (Since we don't collect data from anyone, this is automatically true, but we wanted to call it out explicitly.)

---

## Future features that may change this policy

We are planning two future features that, if added, will change how the app handles data. We'll update this policy and notify users in-app before either feature ships.

**1. Advertising.** A future version of Critterly may show ads from Google AdMob to support development. AdMob may collect advertising identifiers and limited usage data per Google's policies. A one-time "Remove ads" purchase will be available to disable all advertising. Until that release, no ads are present and no advertising data is collected.

**2. Pet sitter sharing.** A future version may allow you to share specific pet schedules with another person (a pet sitter, family member, etc.) for a limited time. This feature would require a backend server, an account system, and would transmit the shared data to the recipient's device. This feature is not built yet, and you would have to opt in to use it.

If and when either feature ships, this Privacy Policy will be updated, and the changes will be summarized in the app's "What's new" section.

---

## Your rights

Because we don't collect or store your data on any server, traditional data-rights requests (access, deletion, portability under GDPR/CCPA) don't really apply — you already have full access to your own data and can delete it at any time via Settings → Reset all data, or by uninstalling the app.

If you have questions about this anyway, email **support@critterly.app**.

---

## Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we'll change the "Last updated" date at the top and surface a notice in-app. Material changes (e.g. introducing ads or accounts) will be flagged prominently.
